Fixed Pseudo 12-Eye List

What Changed

This is a corrected pseudo-12 list built from fixed stronghold eye count and fixed altar chest pearl prediction. The previous published list had known issues with portal eye counts, altar loot seed accounting and enchanted-book RNG consumption.

The fixed scan keeps the same practical definition: a base seed is listed when corrected portal eyes plus predicted altar chest pearls total at least 12. The new list is, as far as empirical testing goes, 100% accurate.

Comparison With the Old List

The old article listed 5,916 candidates. The corrected list has 6,001, a net change of +85.

  • 1,072 old candidates were removed (18.12% of the old list).
  • 1,157 new candidates were added (19.28% of the corrected list).
  • 70 candidates stayed in both lists but had corrected values.
  • 1,549 corrected candidates have no sister found in high32 0..65535 (25.81% of the corrected list).
