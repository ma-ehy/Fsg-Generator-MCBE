Help

This generator will guarantee very good seeds to practice. There are multiple filters available:

Filter Types

Stronghold Village: Guaranteed stronghold village and bastion in the nether.

Stronghold Village (packless): Same as stronghold village, but doesn't require a pack (and therefore has fewer seeds).

Ruined Portal: Guarantees a very close ruined portal with looting 3 sword, 27 iron nuggets, a flint and steel, 8-9 obsidian and a golden axe and pickaxe (with the pack). There is bastion in nether, and the strongholds are guaranteed to be within 2000 blocks from 0,0, which means that educated travel is guaranteed to be good.

Village: Guarantees a village (not stronghold) and lava pool forced by the pack, fastion in nether, stronghold within 2k of 0,0.

Classic: Guarantees stronghold village and insta fort, greatly increased pearl rates for classic.

Structureless: Guarantees mega canyon at spawn for enter and iron ore, bastion in nether, stronghold within 2k from 0,0.

Desert Temple: Guarantees a desert temple spawn with lava pools thanks to the pack and fastion in nether.

For any additional info, requests and feedback, join the Discord.

Ranked Help

Ranked seeds are like fsg, but filtered to be even better in quality and stricter in where the structures generates. The addon will also grant extremely enhanced rng, and - for the ruined portal behavior pack within the addon - will force certain structures to always generate at specific locations. For more information about ranked and the addon, join the discord server.
