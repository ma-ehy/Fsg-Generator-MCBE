What is Bedrock Lockout?

Bedrock Lockout is an add-on that offers a bingo minigame that can be played alone or with friends. You can apply it creating a new world and this will allow you to play a lockout game. The goal of the game is to claim as many tasks as possible as fast as possible or before the opponents. The experience is customizable by being allowed to set a time limit for the run and the number of total tasks, which are chosen randomly from a poll of 197 total tasks. Download the add-on and try it!
