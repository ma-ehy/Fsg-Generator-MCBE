Searching For Pseudo 12-Eyes in 26.50

Introduction

In the update 26.50, Minecraft: Bedrock Edition fixed a bug where the stronghold RNG was offset by other structures, such as villages. Therefore, the only boost in likelihood for us to get 12 eyes near spawn is now gone. With the addition of the 26.50+ SSG category, it is now needed more than ever to find a good, new SSG seed.

The first obvious step is to find possible Pseudo-12 Eyes seeds. To do it, we need to predict the eye count and the altar chest loot of candidate strongholds for each base seed (232). If the sum of the eyes in the portal and pearls in altar chests is equal to or greater than 12, the seed is a candidate.

On The Absence of Trial Chambers

Trial chamber barrels can contain pearls as well, so technically those can get us a pseudo-12 eye too. Furthermore, trial chambers carve out strongholds, but not the portal blocks: therefore, an ideal seed with a completable, exposed portal frame with the rest of the portal room overwritten by the trial chamber is theoretically possible near spawn.

However, due to the complexity of this task, and the absence of a way to seedfind for trial chamber barrel loot yet, I have not seedfound for this.

Results and Limitations

Out of 4,294,967,296 bases, 5916 potential pseudo-12 Eyes bases were found. The majority of these are indeed pseudo-12 eyes. However, due to limitations with the tool I made for this research, there are three main issues:

  • Eye count is not always accurate. From testing, about 1/10 of seeds has the wrong eye count, and lower eye counts appear more prone to this error. Since manual checking was very limited in order to make the list public as soon as possible, these numbers could be completely wrong.
  • Altar chest pearl count is not always accurate: for unknown reasons, sometimes there simply are not the predicted amount of pearls in the chests. This seems rarer than the eye-count error.
  • Enchantment book limitation: enchantment books consume more RNG calls, and because I did not know how to predict that branch, about 3% of the chests could not be predicted accurately.

Searching For the Perfect Seed

This step requires a rather manual search that I am not keen on doing, which means the work of manually checking the best seeds and finding a route for them will be left to those who are more capable of doing so: you, the reader.

However, I processed the data to make this search as easy as possible for runners, by filtering for:

  • stronghold height
  • fortress distance (approx)
  • altar pearl proximity radius (how far away the required pearl chests are)
  • distance from 0 0

Interactive List

Realistically, a sub-2 seed can already be found within these lists.

How to use the list:

  • The first column gives the base seed for each candidate. Red base seeds mean no sister seed found in high32 0..65535.
  • The verified sister column gives one full sister seed whose target stronghold was rechecked. Clicking the sister seed copies the portal-room /tp command. Warning: this is still a generator-level check; cave/terrain edge cases can rarely break the in-game portal room.
  • Use the sliders below to change the weight of each metric: stronghold height, fortress distance, altar pearl proximity radius and distance from 0 0.
